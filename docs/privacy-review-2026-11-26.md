## Confirm with the association before declaring full GDPR compliance

- Confirm registered office, privacy mailbox monitoring, legal representative and formal controller roles; the contact action forwards messages to the Comune's PEC. Confirm whether the Comune is a recipient, joint controller or independent controller; document the arrangement and review the forwarding rule.
- Confirm Article 6 bases per purpose (including reports, volunteer membership, technical logs, user accounts); retention periods for each dataset, backups, hosting logs, mail and WhatsApp, and whether 90-day QR deletion runs in production.
- Confirm Article 28 processor agreements, international transfers and safeguards for hosting, Google Analytics, WhatsApp/Meta, any push service and OpenAI where personal data are submitted; review external requests before consent, including maps, fonts and weather. No assertion that all third-party services are necessary or compliant.
- Newsletter: explicit opt-in is now required for new subscriptions; verify historical subscriber consent, the unsubscribe mailbox and actual removal process. Existing subscription timestamp and technical details are not a complete versioned consent audit. Consider a dedicated consent ledger and confirmed opt-in. Do not treat existing subscribers as newly opted in.
- Cookie banner: verify clean-browser requests before selection, after refusal, after acceptance, and after withdrawal; test all public routes and settings. localStorage remembers the choice; it is not proof of server-side consent record. Confirm data retention and Google Analytics configuration.
- Verify notices at every collection point, rights request procedure, records of processing, data minimization, security controls, backup retention and breach procedures. This is a code review, not a certification or legal opinion.
